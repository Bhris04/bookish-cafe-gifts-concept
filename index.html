Bookish Café Gifts is an independent bookstore with a home at Crafted at the Port of Los Angeles and a mobile bookstore that brings its curated reads and bookish gifts to communities across Southern California.

Whether you stop by in San Pedro or meet the book truck at an event, there’s always a chance to discover something new.
